Get Help & Advice

Dealing with online harassment can be stressful. Here are some immediate steps you can take to protect your mental well-being:

1. Don't Respond

Bullies often look for a reaction. Replying can make things worse. It is okay to walk away from the screen.

2. Block & Restrict

Use platform settings to block the person or restrict comments so they cannot reach you anymore.

3. Save Evidence

Take screenshots of hurtful messages or posts in case you need to show an adult or school authority later.

Learn: Prevention Tips

Help build a kinder digital community for everyone with these simple habits:

Think Before You Post

Ask yourself: Is it true? Is it helpful? Is it kind? If not, think twice before hitting send.

Be an Upstander

If you see someone being bullied online, support them privately or report the content. Don't stay silent.

Protect Your Privacy

Keep your passwords secure and think carefully before sharing personal photos or details online.

🚨 Emergency Help

If you are feeling overwhelmed, scared, or unsafe, please reach out to a trusted adult right away.

Talk to a trusted adult, teacher, school counselor, or parent. You do not have to carry this burden by yourself.
